As you can see in the spritesheet above, ThinkerBox still needs a lot more development. Judging by the theme, you might see why ThinkerBox is called that. Not to sure how it will change yet though. The reason why the third one is missing legs is because I couldn't figure out the best placement for the flames, so I just removed the legs so that I could easily add them. That is everything I have to share right now, as ThinkerBox is still very early in development. While ThinkerBox doesn't have a working prototype, the next project I am working on does.
